What this site sells

Proven work: AI work that arrives with its proof.

When an AI system does consequential work, you get the output and the vendor's word about how it was made. Here the word is replaced by a record: every model call and tool call that formed the work, request and response together, hash-chained on a public ledger whose head is anchored to the drand beacon and the Bitcoin blockchain — plus a door any stranger can open, no key, no account. Reading the record leaves a receipt too.

Test it in sixty seconds: hand any AI you trust one URL and it receives the work's claim, the full evidence payloads, and its own inspection receipt — and can sign a verdict onto the ledger if it stands behind one. Or send one bounded case to build@miscsubjects.com; the first is free. The whole offer, in plain words: what this site sells.

Everything below is the same machinery selling itself. Every article extracts its claims and shows its sources, or says it has none; every revision is logged; any claim can be disputed on the page it appears on, and the dispute is answered on the record. The counts on this page are computed live, never asserted. Machines get the same doors: fetch any page as JSON, invoke a capability, get a receipt. Models will change; the record survives the models that wrote it.

The whole system, through one object

Every step is live — click any of them
  1. 01 An idea becomes an object with one address. Everything after this is that address answering in different forms. The page a person reads →/a/herniated-disc
  2. 02 A model opens the same address and gets structured data — claims, sources, history — not scraped prose. The data a model reads →/api/articles/herniated-disc
  3. 03 The object travels as one folder — page, data, skill, manifest, hash lineage — so it can be carried into any other system whole. The folder it travels as →?bundle=1
  4. 04 Claims carry openable sources — the share that do is published live, not asserted — and any claim can be challenged on its page. Disputes are objects too. The evidence map →/api/metrics/grounding
  5. 05 Acting on the system goes through one door. Any instruction — read, write, run a tool, post, generate — is addressed to the dispatch and returns a receipt. The protocol →/api/dispatch
  6. 06 Every act leaves a receipt in a ledger you can open — what ran, what it sent, what came back. History here is the operating record, not marketing. What is promised and proven →/api/dispatch?registry=1
  7. 07 Failures become laws, laws become skills. When something goes wrong it is written down as an enforced rule, and the rule ships as a skill models load before they work. The system corrects itself in public. The skills, each tied to its failure →/api/skills
  8. 08 The loop closes. Revision changes the object; every projection — page, data, skill, law — updates together. Nothing is rewritten, so nothing drifts. The full specification →/api/articles/system-map
